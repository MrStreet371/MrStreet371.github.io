My Research

Lithium-6 in an extremely Li-rich, very metal-poor giant

Master's thesis · Uppsala University · 2026 – present

Standard Big Bang nucleosynthesis predicts virtually no 6Li in old, very metal-poor stars, yet Sitnova et al. (2023) reported a 6Li detection in a Li-rich, very metal-poor giant using 1D non-LTE models. I am reanalysing this star with self-consistent 3D non-LTE spectral synthesis (Stagger model atmospheres and the Balder radiative-transfer code), which capture the convective line asymmetries that can mimic the 6Li signature.

The 6Li/7Li ratio is fitted to high-resolution Subaru/HDS spectra with Bayesian MCMC, and the 3D calculations run on the Dardel supercomputer (NAISS). The project started as a course project supervised by Karin Lind and Ella Xi Wang and is now being extended in my Master's thesis.

Variable-range hopping in granular metals

Bachelor's thesis · University of Murcia · 2024

Monte Carlo simulations (Metropolis–Hastings) of electron transport by variable-range hopping in granular metals, studying how the conductivity of these disordered systems depends on temperature.
