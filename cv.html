Master course project, Uppsala University
Detecting Lithium-6: a spectroscopic reanalysis of an extremely Li-rich very metal-poor star
Supervisors: Dr. Karin Lind and Dr. Ella Xi Wang
  • Reanalysis of the only reported 6Li detection in a Li-rich very metal-poor giant, previously based on 1D NLTE models, using self-consistent 3D NLTE spectral synthesis (Stagger 3D model atmospheres + Balder radiative transfer).
  • Ran and optimised the 3D NLTE calculations on the Dardel supercomputer (NAISS): model resolution, snapshot sampling and abundance grid.
  • Bayesian fitting of the Li I 6707 Å and 6103 Å lines of Subaru/HDS spectra with MCMC (emcee), finding a substantially lower 6Li/7Li ratio than previously reported.

Report available on request.

Sep – Dec 2024
Research Assistant, Uppsala University
Detector rate simulations of pair-annihilation neutrinos produced by neutrino-cooled stars
Supervisors: Dr. Andreas Korn and Dr. Erin O'Sullivan
  • Time-dependent rates of e+e− pair-annihilation neutrinos of all flavours, including MSW oscillations, for a 20 M☉ star in JUNO, KamLAND and Super-Kamiokande-Gd.
  • Best case: detection up to six months before core collapse at 0.2 kpc; not feasible beyond 10 kpc.
